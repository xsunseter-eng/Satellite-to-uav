================================================================================
PROJECT PURPOSE: SATELLITE-TO-UAV SYNTHETIC FLIGHT VIDEO GENERATOR
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The purpose of this project is to build a configurable pipeline that generates
realistic synthetic Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) flight videos directly from
standardized public satellite and overhead geospatial data (multispectral/IR
imagery + aligned 3D Digital Surface Models).

By synthesizing controlled, ground-truth-annotated UAV footage across diverse
terrains, spectral modalities, camera geometries, lighting conditions, and
sensor/environmental degradations, this project provides scalable training,
testing, and benchmarking data for aerial computer vision, visual navigation,
cross-view geo-localization, and perception robustness.


2. CORE FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Standardized Input & Origin-Centered Batch Generation
- Accept a standardized georeferenced data package (GeoTIFF / Cloud-Optimized
  GeoTIFF [COG] imagery with CRS metadata, multispectral/IR bands, and aligned
  DSM/DTM elevation layers), streamed/clipped on demand along flight corridors.
- Take a target geographic origin point and geofence radius (constrained to
  100 km <= radius <= 300 km, strictly land-focused).
- Generate N distinct synthetic UAV flight videos per run alongside structured
  flight telemetry and parameter ground-truth metadata.

2.2 Randomized Flight Kinematics & Trajectories
For each of the N generated videos, randomize initial flight parameters within
predetermined, bounded ranges:
- Starting Position : Randomly sampled within a maximum distance of X km from
                      the origin point (bounded by the region geofence).
- Orientation (Yaw) : Randomly sampled initial heading along the yaw axis.
- Flight Speed      : Randomly sampled from a predetermined [v_min, v_max] range.
- Flight Altitude   : Randomly sampled from a predetermined [h_min, h_max] range.

2.3 Configurable & Limitable Variation Parameters

[Baseline Variations]
- Sun Angle / Illumination : Varying solar azimuth & elevation (shadow length
                             and direction) + day/night relighting priors.
- Spectral Camera Models   : Virtual sensors covering available spectral bands
                             (RGB, Panchromatic/Grayscale, NIR, multispectral
                             composites, and simulated thermal/IR views).
- Field of View (FoV)      : Varying camera horizontal/vertical FoV angles.
- Camera Pitch Direction   : Varying gimbal pitch (nadir to oblique forward).
- Footage Errors           : Synthetic lens/footage defects such as lens cracks
                             and water marks / droplets.

[Approved Extended Variations (v1)]
- Motion Blur & Rolling Shutter : Speed/altitude/angular-rate-coupled directional
                                  blur and CMOS rolling-shutter skew.
- Optical Lens Distortion       : Radial & tangential distortion (k1, k2, p1, p2)
                                  emulating wide/telephoto UAV optics.
- Auto-Exposure & DR Clipping   : Highlight blowout on bright surfaces, shadow
                                  crushing, and exposure adaptation lag.
- Sensor Noise Model            : Optical ISO/photon shot noise and thermal/IR
                                  Fixed-Pattern Noise (FPN) / striping.
- Atmospheric Haze & Visibility : Distance- and altitude-dependent aerial haze,
                                  fog, and reduced visibility scattering.
- Sun Glare & Specular Glint    : Optical lens flare toward the sun and specular
                                  glint off water, glass, and metallic roofs.
- Color Temp & White Balance    : Warm/cool illumination shifts and drifting
                                  camera white balance.
- Video Output Resolution       : Varying native output resolutions (e.g., 480p,
                                  720p, 1080p, 4K) without digital zoom crop.

2.4 Multi-Region & Multi-Difficulty Geofence Policy
- Every target region must satisfy:
  * Minimum geofence radius : 100 km (smaller coverage regions are rejected)
  * Maximum geofence radius : 300 km (larger regions are capped at 300 km)
  * Land-focused origins    : Geofences prioritized for ~100% land coverage
- Selected / Candidate Regions (see REGIONS_AND_PARAMS.json & SATELLITE_DATASETS.md):
  [Confirmed US Regions — NAIP + USGS 3DEP + Satellite Priors]
  1. South Georgia / North Florida (Jacksonville Land-Focused Corridor, USA) — r = 120 km
  2. Las Vegas, NV (USA) — Urban-to-Desert Edge & Night Contrast             — r = 120 km
  3. West/Central Mojave Desert, CA (USA) — Feature-Sparse Desert            — r = 110 km
  4. Moab / Canyonlands, UT (USA) — Steep Canyons & High Relief              — r = 300 km

  [Confirmed Non-US Regions — Nationwide Open Sub-Meter RGB+NIR + LiDAR]
  5. Central Spain: Madrid–Toledo–La Mancha (Spain, IGN PNOA)                — r = 200 km
     (Arid plateau + low-contrast urban; replaces cancelled Khartoum)
  6. Paris–Loire Industrial Basin: Orléans–Paris (France, IGN BD ORTHO+HD)   — r = 180 km
     (European industrial river ports + dense urban; replaces cancelled Rotterdam)
